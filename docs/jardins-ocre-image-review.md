## Generation and delivery

Tool: built-in Imagegen. All selected outputs are generative edits of supplied photographs, not newly captured photographs. Requested natural material textures, restrained color and exposure, small evidence-based camera changes and preservation of the site's identity. Original garden imperfections and active work stages were retained where visible. Despite these constraints, reconstruction can alter details; the site disclosure states this explicitly.

Exact initial prompts and source references: `jardins-ocre-image-prompts.json`. Final PNG-to-WebP mapping and the corrected source-10 prompt: `jardins-ocre-image-outputs.json`. The rejected source-10 version is not used on the website. Original tool outputs were retained.

Final directory: `public/images/projects/jardins-ocre/`. Fourteen 1536 × 1024 WebP images, quality 90. Only the cover loads with priority; gallery images retain Next.js lazy loading and responsive sizes. Gallery order moves from overviews to details, then preparation, planting and maintenance.

Project: `/projets/jardins-ocre`, also in the homepage carousel. Title is provisional, location unconfirmed. Copy describes visible garden elements and work only; no client, dates, measurements or completion status inferred.
